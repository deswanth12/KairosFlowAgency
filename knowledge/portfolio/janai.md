# PROJECT: JanAI Citizen Welfare Platform

- **project_name**: JanAI Citizen Welfare Platform
- **category**: AI & Automation
- **client**: Public Services / State Welfare Pilot
- **industry**: GovTech & Public Welfare
- **timeline**: 10 Weeks
- **problem**: Citizens faced friction understanding state welfare schemes, eligibility rules, and required verification documents.
- **solution**: Modular welfare intelligence engine with regional language understanding, automated scheme matching, and checklist generators.
- **features**: Dynamic Scheme Matcher, Multilingual Regional Voice & Text, Automated Verification Checklist.
- **technologies**: JavaScript, React, Node.js, NLP APIs, Tailwind CSS.
- **verified_result**: <10s Eligibility Resolution, Pilot-Ready for Andhra Pradesh State Deployment.

# PROJECT: Lumina Legal AI & Workflow Engine

- **project_name**: Lumina Legal Intake
- **category**: AI & Automation
- **client**: Lumina Global Advisory
- **industry**: Legal Tech & Enterprise SaaS
- **timeline**: 10 Weeks
- **problem**: Corporate attorneys were spending 18+ hours per week manually redlining standard contracts and hunting across internal drives for precedence clauses.
- **solution**: Kairos Flow engineered a localized retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system with custom clause risk classifiers, automated Slack/Teams review triggers, and deep CRM synchronization.
- **features**:
  - Precision clause extraction & indemnification risk grading
  - Interactive redline assistant grounded in precedent briefs
  - Multi-channel dispatch to Slack, Teams, and document management systems
- **technologies**: Python, FastAPI, LangChain, OpenAI GPT-4o, pgvector, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **deliverables**: Deployed AI backend, vector embeddings pipeline, web app, operations dashboard.
- **verified_result**:
  - 82% reduction in first-pass contract review time
  - 100% citation traceability back to original document pages
  - 14,000+ legal documents processed monthly
- **case_study_url**: https://kairosflow.agency/work/lumina-intelligent-intake

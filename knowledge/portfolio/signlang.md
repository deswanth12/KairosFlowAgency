# PROJECT: SignLang AI Vision Translator

- **project_name**: SignLang AI Vision Translator
- **category**: AI & Automation
- **client**: Accessibility & Assistive Tech
- **industry**: Computer Vision & Healthcare Accessibility
- **timeline**: 6 Weeks
- **problem**: Communication barriers for hearing-impaired individuals where sign language interpreters are unavailable.
- **solution**: Real-time computer vision translator using Google MediaPipe and Flask to map 3D hand joints and synthesize audible speech.
- **features**: 42 3D Hand Landmark Tracking, Sentence Accumulator, Web Speech Audio Output.
- **technologies**: Python, MediaPipe, OpenCV, Flask, Web Speech API, TensorFlow.
- **verified_result**: 30+ FPS Vision Speed, 96.4% Classification Accuracy, Runs on Standard Webcams.
- **github_url**: https://github.com/deswanth12/singlangbydeshu
- **case_study_url**: https://kairosflow.agency/work/signlang-ai-computer-vision-translator

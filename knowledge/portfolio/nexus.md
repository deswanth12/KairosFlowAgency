# PROJECT: Nexus Data Cloud Growth Campaign

- **project_name**: Nexus B2B Growth Campaign
- **category**: Digital Marketing
- **client**: Nexus Cloud Infrastructure
- **industry**: Cloud Computing & DevOps
- **timeline**: 16 Weeks
- **problem**: High customer acquisition costs on paid search (> $1,400 CAC) due to broad keyword targeting and low landing page conversion rates (1.1%).
- **solution**: Kairos Flow engineered targeted high-intent landing pages, rebuilt the technical SEO architecture, developed an interactive ROI cloud migration calculator, and restructured LinkedIn/Google ad funnels.
- **features**:
  - Interactive AWS/GCP total cost of ownership calculator
  - 18 high-authority architectural breakdown guides ranking in top 3 Google positions
  - Server-side multi-touch conversion attribution linking CRM revenue
- **technologies**: Google Ads, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Next.js, HubSpot CRM, PostHog
- **deliverables**: Landing pages, ad creatives, SEO content matrix, attribution reporting.
- **verified_result**:
  - -54% reduction in qualified Cost-Per-Lead (CPL)
  - +210% organic search traffic growth
  - $4.2M new attributable pipeline generated

# Agency Values & Principles — Kairos Flow

1. **Strategy First**
   We understand the client's business model, customer dynamics, and commercial goals before writing code or producing media.

2. **Built In-House by Specialists**
   Design, full-stack engineering, AI pipelines, and video content are executed directly by the core leadership team. Zero outsourcing.

3. **Clear & Rapid Communication**
   One direct point of contact. Dedicated Slack/WhatsApp collaboration channels, async video updates, and weekly staging environment previews.

4. **Practical Technology Over Hype**
   We deploy robust, battle-tested modern tools (Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Python, DaVinci Resolve) rather than chasing fragile trends.

5. **Architectural & Purpose-Driven Design**
   Restrained, timeless, and editorial. We design for clarity, conversion, and longevity.

# Pricing & Quotation Policies

## Core Pricing Rules
1. **Indicative Ranges Only**: All numbers shared by the AI Consultant or preliminary estimators are indicative reference ranges. 
2. **Never Final Quotation**: The AI Consultant must never promise a binding final quotation. Final proposals are prepared by Founder Desvanth and the team following formal discovery.
3. **Fixed-Scope Milestones**: We quote fixed prices per milestone sprint to protect clients from unexpected billing overruns.
4. **Payment Structure**: Typically structured as 30% upfront deposit to initiate Discovery & Architecture, 40% upon staging build milestone review, and 30% upon final production deployment and IP handover.
5. **No Proprietary Lock-In**: 100% IP, codebase, design files, and cloud accounts are transferred to the client upon milestone completion.

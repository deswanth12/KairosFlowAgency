# Frequently Asked Questions — Kairos Flow Agency

## 1. Timeline
- **Question**: How long does a website take to design and develop?
- **Answer**: Typical business website delivery takes 3 to 8 weeks depending on project scope, custom features, and backend integrations. Complex SaaS dashboards and mobile applications take 6 to 12 weeks.

## 2. Pricing
- **Question**: How much does a website or digital product cost?
- **Answer**: Our business website projects start from ₹35,000 / $500. The final price depends on custom features, number of pages, third-party integrations, and timeline. All projects are quoted with fixed-scope milestones so you never face surprise costs.

## 3. Remote & Global Work
- **Question**: Do you work with businesses outside your city or country?
- **Answer**: Yes. We work remotely with clients across India, the United States, the UK, and the UAE. We coordinate projects through dedicated Slack/WhatsApp channels, video discovery sessions, and weekly staging walkthroughs.

## 4. Revisions & Approvals
- **Question**: What is your policy on revisions?
- **Answer**: Our 6-stage framework requires explicit client approval at every stage (Discover, Plan, Design, Build, Launch). You review and approve design wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma before development begins, ensuring zero misalignment.

## 5. Post-Launch Support
- **Question**: What happens after the project launches?
- **Answer**: Every project includes a 30-day post-launch warranty covering bug fixes and adjustments. We also offer ongoing maintenance retainers for continuous feature updates and growth marketing.

## 6. Code & Asset Ownership
- **Question**: Who owns the source code and designs?
- **Answer**: You own 100% of the intellectual property, source code, Figma design files, raw 4K video footage, and cloud accounts upon project completion. There is zero vendor lock-in.
